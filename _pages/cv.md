## Research Experience
* [Robot Ecology Laboratory](https://faculty.sites.uci.edu/robotecology/) (Irvine, CA), Graduate Student Researcher, Sept. 2022 - Present
  * ***Advisors:*** Magnus Egerstedt and Faryar Jabbari
      * Investigating why, how, and when heterogeneous robots should collaborate through the expansion of relevant sets, a hybrid automata-based framework, and the satisfaction of specific pairwise conditions, by applying tools from control theory. 
      * Explored the application of ecological principles in robotics to understand how the environment can influence when robots should work together.
      * Led the assembly and programming of differential-wheeled and mecanum-wheeled robots for the UCI Robotarium, overseeing task delegation among undergraduate and graduate lab members.
      * Conducted lab tours, which include live demonstrations of mobile robots performing coordinated behaviors, to 500+ visitors (e.g., K-12 student field trips, engineering outreach events, and visiting researchers).

* Multi-Robot Systems Laboratory (Monterrey, Nuevo Leon, Mexico), Visiting Researcher, Oct. 2023 - Nov. 2023
  * ***Advisor:*** Luis Guerrero-Bonilla
    * Developed a scalable, pairwise collaboration architecture, consisting of a centralized queue to determine pairwise arrangements between robots and a finite state machine for decision-making, enabling individual robots to enhance their functionality.
    * Supported graduate and undergraduate students with programming the Dingo-O robot that was deployed in the ITESM Robotarium.
    * Designed a mechanical structure for integration onto the Dingo-O robot.

* [Autonomous Systems, Perception, Intelligence, and Navigation Laboratory](https://ece.osu.edu/aspin) (Irvine, CA), Graduate Student Researcher, Sept. 2020 - June 2022
  * ***Advisor:*** Zak Kassas
    * Spearheaded the opportunistic aerial vehicle navigation projects, which involved developing computationally efficient transmitter selection strategies and analyzing the performance of aerial vehicles using an inertial navigation system and ambient cellular signals in GPS-denied environments.
    * Specified the equipment needing to purchase for unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) flight experiments for the lab.
    * Collaborated with graduate students to mount and configure communication electronics, such as antennas and receivers, for flight experiments with a UAV.
    * Processed and analyzed data collected by the UAV from cellular towers during flight experiments.

* [Biological Control Laboratory](https://yeung.me.ucsb.edu/) (Santa Barbara, CA), Undergraduate Research Assistant, Sept. 2019 - Mar. 2020
  * ***Advisor:*** Enoch Yeung
    * Investigated new data-driven methods for synthesis of genetic circuits for nonlinear biological systems.
    * Prepared synthetic biology experiments and performed data collection for graduate students and a junior research associate.
