## Research Experience
* [Robot Ecology Laboratory](https://faculty.sites.uci.edu/robotecology/) (Irvine, CA), Graduate Student Researcher, Sept. 2022 - Present
  * ***Advisors:*** Magnus Egerstedt and Faryar Jabbari
      * Investigating why, how, and when heterogeneous multi-robot teams should collaborate—through the expansion of relevant sets, a hybrid automata-based framework, and the satisfaction of specific pairwise conditions—by leveraging ecological principles and control theory. 
      * Organize and supervise robot-building sessions, overseeing task delegation among undergraduate and graduate lab members.
      * Contribute to assembling and programming the differential-wheeled and mecanum-wheeled robots that are deployed in the UCI Robotarium. 
      * Lead lab tours, which include live demonstrations of mobile robots performing coordinated behaviors, to 300+ visitors.

* Multi-Robot Systems Laboratory (Monterrey, Nuevo Leon, Mexico), Visiting Researcher, Oct. 2023 - Nov. 2023
  * ***Advisor:*** Luis Guerrero-Bonilla
    * Developed a scalable, pairwise collaboration architecture that allows for large teams of heterogeneous robots to enhance their functionalities when working together.
    * Supported graduate and undergraduate students with programming the Dingo-O robot, which was deployed in the ITESM Robotarium.
    * Performed analysis and then designed a mechanical structure in SolidWorks for integration with the Dingo-O robot.

* [Autonomous Systems, Perception, Intelligence, and Navigation Laboratory](https://ece.osu.edu/aspin) (Irvine, CA), Graduate Student Researcher, Sept. 2020 - June 2022
  * ***Advisor:*** Zak Kassas
    * Spearheaded the terrestrial-based opportunistic aerial vehicle navigation projects, which involved developing computationally efficient transmitter selection strategies and conducting performance analyses of aerial vehicle navigation with cellular signals in GPS-denied environments.
    * Specified the equipment needing to purchase for unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) flight experiments for the lab.
    * Collaborated with graduate students to mount equipment (e.g., antennas and receiver) on a UAV and configure its software for flight experiments.
    * Processed and analyzed data collected by the UAV from cellular towers during flight experiments.

* [Biological Control Laboratory](https://yeung.me.ucsb.edu/) (Santa Barbara, CA), Undergraduate Research Assistant, Sept. 2019 - Mar. 2020
  * ***Advisor:*** Enoch Yeung
    * Investigated new data-driven methods for synthesis of genetic circuits for nonlinear biological systems.
