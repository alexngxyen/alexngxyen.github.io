## Research Experience
* [UCI Robot Ecology Laboratory](https://faculty.sites.uci.edu/robotecology/) (Irvine, CA), Graduate Student Researcher, Sept. 2022 - Present
  * ***Advisors:*** Magnus Egerstedt and Faryar Jabbari
      * Investigate why, how, and when different types of robots should collaborate through the expansion/contraction of reachable sets, a hybrid automata-based framework, and the satisfaction of specific pairwise conditions, leveraging tools from control theory and optimization.
      * Explored the application of ecological principles in robotics to understand how the environment can influence when robots should work together.
      * Led the assembly and programming of differential- and mecanum-wheeled robots in the UCI Robotarium, overseeing task delegation among 12 undergraduate and graduate lab members.
      * Conducted lab tours, which include live demonstrations of mobile robots performing coordinated behaviors, to 500+ visitors (e.g., K-12 student field trips, engineering outreach programs, UCI alumni events, and visiting researchers).

* Multi-Robot Systems Laboratory (Monterrey, Nuevo Leon, Mexico), Visiting Researcher, Oct. 2023 - Nov. 2023
  * ***Advisor:*** Luis Guerrero-Bonilla
    * Designed a scalable, pairwise collaboration architecture, comprised of a centralized queue to determine pairwise arrangements between robots and a finite state machine for decision-making, enabling individual robots to enhance their functionality
    * Collaborated with four graduate and undergraduate students to build and program the Dingo-O robot that was deployed in the ITESM Robotarium.
    * Designed a mechanical structure for integration onto the Dingo-O robot.

* [Autonomous Systems, Perception, Intelligence, and Navigation Laboratory](https://ece.osu.edu/aspin) (Irvine, CA), Graduate Student Researcher, Sept. 2020 - June 2022
  * **Advisor:** Zak Kassas
    * Spearheaded the opportunistic aerial vehicle navigation projects, which involved developing computationally efficient transmitter selection strategies and evaluating the navigation performance of aerial vehicles using an inertial navigation system and ambient cellular signals in GPS-denied environments.
    * Processed and analyzed data collected by an uncrewed aerial vehicle (UAV) from cellular towers during flight experiments.
    * Assisted graduate students in mounting and configuring communication electronics, such as antennas and receivers, for flight experiments with a UAV

* [Biological Control Laboratory](https://yeung.me.ucsb.edu/) (Santa Barbara, CA), Undergraduate Research Assistant, Sept. 2019 - Mar. 2020
  * ***Advisor:*** Enoch Yeung
    * Investigated new data-driven methods for synthesis of genetic circuits for nonlinear biological systems.
    * Prepared synthetic biology experiments and performed data collection for two graduate students and a junior research associate.
